The idea behind the work

Markets are usually last to the party.

Every security is held by the people most optimistic about it — the ones who assume the risk won't reach their holding until it demonstrably does. That's why prices levitate while a threat builds, and why the correction is abrupt. My job is to find what the marginal buyer and seller are failing to price, model each actor's incentives, and frame the call as reward-to-risk — then say plainly what would prove me wrong.
