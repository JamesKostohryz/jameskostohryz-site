James A. Kostohryz is an independent global portfolio strategist with more than 25 years of experience in global financial markets. After a successful career at major buy-side firms and proprietary trading desks, he has been running his own independent research and consulting practice since 2009.

Seven areas of expertise

  1. Global investment strategy. Identification of mega-trends and geopolitical drivers.
  2. Business-cycle analysis. Anticipation of macroeconomic turning points.
  3. Fundamental currency valuation. Exploitation of relative-value cycles.
  4. Equity valuation. Individual security analysis through Real Value Analysis (RVA).
  5. Global cyclicals. Oil & Gas, Banks & Financials, Industrials, and Mining & Basic Materials.
  6. Emerging markets. Deep expertise in Latin America.
  7. Risk management. Low individual-position VaR, uncorrelated factor allocation, and a low beta.
